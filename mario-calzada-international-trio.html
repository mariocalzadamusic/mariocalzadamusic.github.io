Mario Calzada International Group

Improvisation, composition and collective authorship

Mario Calzada performs, composes, and releases his own music regularly in trio, quartet, and quintet formats. Within these formations, he collaborates with different musicians in order to create and perform music at the highest artistic level.

Each ensemble is conceived as a flexible platform, shaped by the musicians involved, where composition, improvisation, and interaction coexist as part of a shared musical language.

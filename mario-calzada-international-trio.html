Mario Calzada International Trio

Improvisation, composition and collective authorship

The Mario Calzada International Trio is a flexible ensemble bringing together musicians from different backgrounds to explore contemporary jazz, free improvisation and composed structures. The project focuses on interaction, sound identity and real-time decision making.

Repertoire includes original compositions by Mario Calzada alongside open-form frameworks developed collaboratively within the ensemble and jazz standard repertoire.

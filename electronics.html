Electronics

An ongoing exploration of sound, synthesis, improvisation, and electronic systems.

Electronics is a space where I explore new relationships between composition, improvisation, and technology. Through synthesis, digital processing, controllers, and real-time interaction, I investigate how electronic systems can become instruments for musical discovery.

These experiments are connected to my broader artistic practice, where structure and freedom coexist. Rather than using electronics only as a tool, I am interested in creating environments where sound can evolve through interaction and decision-making.

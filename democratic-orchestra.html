The Democratic Orchestra

Collective composition, improvisation and political imagination

The Democratic Orchestra is a large-scale collaborative project bringing together performers from different national, cultural and musical backgrounds. The work explores democracy as a sonic practice: negotiation, responsibility, freedom and listening.

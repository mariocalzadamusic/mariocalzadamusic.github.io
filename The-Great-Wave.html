The Great Wave

Solo drum composition · 12’

The Great Wave is a solo drum composition by Mario Calzada, inspired by the Zen story of Ōnami and the metaphor of the wave as a force of transformation. The work explores control and surrender, violence and stillness, form and impermanence, through extended percussion techniques and physical dramaturgy.

Concept

The piece takes its title from the Zen story of Ōnami, a warrior whose name means “Great Wave.” Rather than resisting the wave, Ōnami learns to move with it — transforming danger into strength.

The Great Wave translates this philosophy into sound and gesture. The drum set becomes both an instrument and a body in motion, where rhythm emerges from physical tension, instability, and release. Time is elastic, form is porous, and repetition functions as a ritual rather than a structure.

The work reflects on the relationship between discipline and freedom, echoing martial arts, Zen practice, and improvisation as embodied knowledge.

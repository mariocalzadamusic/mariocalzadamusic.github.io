Mario Calzada International Quintet

Large-form writing, improvisation and contemporary jazz

The Mario Calzada International Quintet extends the quartet format into a more orchestral and formally complex ensemble. The quintet enables extended harmonic language, contrapuntal writing and expanded improvisational roles while maintaining a strong collective identity.

The repertoire combines fully notated compositions with open structures, encouraging risk, interaction and shared responsibility among performers.

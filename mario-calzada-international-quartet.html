Mario Calzada International Quartet

Expanded ensemble, composition and improvisation

The Mario Calzada International Quartet expands the trio format into a broader sonic and structural space, allowing for richer harmonic material, layered textures and extended formal development. The ensemble balances written composition with improvisation and collective interaction.

The project functions as a platform for contemporary jazz and experimental music, integrating influences from composed concert music, free improvisation and rhythmic research.

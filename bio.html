Bio

Mario Calzada is a musician, creator, performer and educator working across contemporary composition, improvisation, and jazz.

Mario Calzada is a contemporary composer, improviser, drummer and educator whose work explores sound, form and collective creation. His artistic practice moves fluidly between written composition, improvisation and performance, engaging with contemporary concert music, jazz and interdisciplinary projects.

His projects often focus on collaboration, social imagination and performative responsibility, questioning traditional hierarchies between composer, performer and listener. Through both artistic research and live performance, he develops formats that invite shared authorship and critical listening.

As an educator, Mario is committed to experimental pedagogies that connect technical practice with artistic identity, fostering curiosity, autonomy and critical reflection in young musicians.

Bio

Mario Calzada is a musician, creator, performer and educator working across contemporary composition, improvisation, and jazz.

Mario Calzada is a composer, jazz drummer, and educator whose artistic practice focuses on making performers and audiences aware of their freedom through the use of improvisational tools. Working at the intersection of composition, improvisation, and collective creation, his music treats sound as a space for choice, responsibility, and critical awareness.

His work spans contemporary classical music, jazz, and experimental practices, often combining notated structures with open forms that invite performers to actively shape the musical outcome. Improvisation functions in his work not as decoration, but as a central compositional strategy through which freedom, authorship, and decision-making are explored. He is the creator of The Democratic Orchestra, an international project developed in Maastricht that brings together musicians of diverse nationalities to investigate collective freedom and shared authorship through music. His solo drum work The Great Wave, inspired by Zen philosophy, reflects on awareness and control within physical and musical action.

Calzada’s work has been presented by festivals and institutions including November Music Festival, Intro in Situ, Siena Jazz, Jazz in the Park, and Transilvania Jazz Festival and many more. He holds a Master’s degree in Classical Composition and is currently a lecturer at Zuyd Conservatorium Maastricht, where he teaches, critical listening, artistic research and many more, guiding students toward autonomy, creativity, and informed artistic decision-making.
